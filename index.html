Who we are

Clear guidance. Responsible action. Realistic expectations.

Game Changer Cyber Retrieve supports people and organizations dealing with digital security incidents and online fraud concerns. We take a case-by-case approach, combining investigation support, appropriate technical assistance, recovery guidance and coordination with independent specialists when needed.

We aim to communicate clearly, treat information carefully and distinguish what can be assessed from what cannot be guaranteed. Any access to a device, account or system must be authorized by its owner.

How we work

A considered process, one step at a time.

Every enquiry is reviewed individually. We do not promise a particular investigation finding or recovery result.

STEP 01

Listen & understand

We ask for a general description of the incident and identify what information may be relevant. Do not send passwords, seed phrases or authentication codes.

STEP 02

Assess & explain

We review the information that can safely be shared, clarify what support may be suitable and explain scope, limitations, fees and any third-party involvement before work begins.

STEP 03

Support & next steps

Depending on the case, we provide agreed guidance or technical assistance, help organize findings, or discuss referral or coordination with an independent specialist.

Important information about recovery assistance

We cannot guarantee recovery of cryptocurrency, funds, accounts or digital assets. Blockchain transactions may be irreversible, and outcomes depend on the facts, available evidence, platform policies and actions of relevant institutions. We do not claim to be a bank, exchange, law-enforcement agency or government authority. Any independent specialist is a separate provider, and their services and terms should be checked directly.
